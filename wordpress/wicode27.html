WiCoDE27 brings research scholars, students, industry leaders and the global ACM-W leadership together in Dubai for two days of mentoring, real industry challenges and recognition. It is the second edition of the ACM Celebration of Women in Computing organised by the Dubai ACM-W Professional Chapter, and this year it opens with the ACM-W Scholars’ Cohort for research scholars from the UAE and abroad.

Day 1, Wednesday 7 April: ACM-W Scholars’ Cohort. For research scholars from all disciplines. Industry panel, Pitch to Industry, Coffee with a Chair, Career Studio and the WiCoDE Recognition Awards.

Day 2, Thursday 8 April: WiCoDE27 Celebration. For students and student chapters. Industry Challenge Sprint finals, hands-on workshops, the chapter showcase and the student awards.

WiCoDE is the ACM Celebration of Women in Computing organised in Dubai by the Dubai ACM-W Professional Chapter with the Dubai ACM Professional Chapter, hosted by BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus. It brings together students, research scholars, faculty, industry professionals and ACM-W leaders from around the world to highlight the contributions of women in computing and to build an inclusive and collaborative computing community across the UAE.

Lab to Launch. Research is where ideas begin. WiCoDE27 is about what comes next: taking research from the lab to industry, a thesis to a career, and classroom learning to real problems. Decode the problem. Create the solution. Lead the change.

The first edition. WiCoDE26 took place on 16 and 17 February 2026 at BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus. It brought together global ACM-W leadership, faculty members, industry professionals and ACM student chapters, with 96 registered participants from institutions across the UAE. Read about WiCoDE26.

About ACM-W. ACM-W, the ACM Council on Women in Computing, supports, celebrates and advocates internationally for the full engagement of women in all aspects of the computing field. ACM Celebrations of Women in Computing are regional events held around the world with participants from academia, industry and government.

Who should attend: research scholars from all disciplines, in the UAE and abroad; undergraduate and postgraduate students; faculty members and researchers; industry professionals and practitioners; ACM and ACM-W members and student chapter leaders.

Highlights: Pitch to Industry, three minutes to show industry what your research can do. Industry Challenge Sprint, real problems from real companies: win an interview, not a voucher. My Research in 60 Seconds, your research in one vertical video. Coffee with a Chair, ten minutes at the table with ACM-W leaders and industry experts. Career Studio, with a professional headshot, LinkedIn review and CV clinic, free for every participant. And the WiCoDE Recognition Awards.

Pitch to Industry+

WhoResearch scholars from any discipline.

SubmitA one-page abstract covering the problem, your research, how it could be applied in industry, and what you would need from an industry partner. Published or under-review work is welcome.

DatesAbstracts by 28 February 2027; ten finalists announced on 15 March 2027.

On the dayA three-minute pitch and two minutes of live feedback from the industry panel on 7 April.

RecognitionBest Pitch award and three months of mentoring with an industry leader or ACM-W chair.

My Research in 60 Seconds+

HowPost a vertical video of up to 60 seconds explaining your research on LinkedIn or Instagram with the hashtag #WiCoDE27, tag the Dubai ACM-W Professional Chapter, and submit the link through the entry form.

DatesEntries from 1 to 28 February 2027; public voting from 1 to 10 March 2027.

RecognitionThe top ten videos are shown at the event and the jury selects the Best Research Video.

Industry Challenge Sprint+

WhoTeams of three or four students from UAE universities.

HowPartner companies release real problem statements on 1 February 2027. Teams work for five to six weeks, with one online check-in with the company.

DatesFive finalist teams announced on 15 March 2027; finals on 8 April 2027.

PrizeAn interview or a shadow day at the partner company.

Campus Cup+

The six partner student chapters compete for the Outstanding Student Chapter award. Chapters earn points for registered participants, event content they publish, entries to the challenges and their stand at the chapter showcase on 8 April.

Call for Workshops+

We invite proposals for hands-on workshops of 75 to 90 minutes, held in parallel tracks on 8 April. Send a title, a 200-word description, the intended audience and the facilitator’s short bio by 15 January 2027.

Volunteer+

Students from the partner chapters can volunteer for registration, speaker support, the Career Studio and social media. Volunteers receive a certificate and Campus Cup points for their chapter.

ACM-W Scholars’ Cohort

The ACM-W Scholars’ Cohort on 7 April 2027 brings research scholars from all disciplines together with ACM-W leaders and industry research experts. Every year, a group of research scholars from the UAE and abroad is selected as ACM-W Scholars and receives one-on-one feedback on their research, priority access to industry sessions and, for international scholars, the chance of a travel grant.

Applications open 15 November 2026

  • 2027 Cohort

    Who can apply

    • Doctoral and research master’s scholars in any discipline, not only computing.
    • Scholars from universities in the UAE and from any other country.
    • Applicants of any gender are welcome. The programme gives particular attention to women in research.
  • 2027 Cohort

    What ACM-W Scholars receive

    • Selection as an ACM-W Scholar 2027, with a certificate signed by the ACM-W chairs.
    • One-on-one thesis feedback: a 20-minute review of your two-page research summary by a senior mentor who has read it in advance.
    • Priority places in Pitch to Industry and Coffee with a Chair, and access to the Career Studio.
    • Travel grants for international scholars: about 20 grants covering a return flight and three hotel nights, subject to funding.
    • The International Programme: a welcome reception on 6 April and an industry and innovation visit on 9 April.
    • Three months of mentoring after the event, a feature in the ACM-W newsletter, and an invitation to return as a peer mentor in 2028.
  • 2027 Cohort

    How to apply

    1. 1Complete the application form by 15 January 2027 with your CV, a research statement of up to 300 words, your institution and supervisor, and whether you are requesting a travel grant.
    2. 2Ask your supervisor or your ACM-W chapter chair to send a short supporting statement through the form link we email to you.
    3. 3Selected scholars and travel grants are announced on 10 February 2027. International scholars receive invitation and visa support letters by 28 February 2027.
    4. 4Selected scholars submit a two-page research summary by 15 March 2027 and are matched with their mentor by 20 March 2027.
    5. 5Join us in Dubai from 6 to 9 April 2027.

  • Selection criteria

    Quality and clarity of the research, its potential impact, the applicant’s motivation, and a balance of disciplines, institutions and countries.

  • Not selected?

    All research scholars are welcome to register for Day 1, submit a Pitch to Industry abstract, enter the video challenge and book Coffee with a Chair.

How to nominate

Nominations are open from 1 December 2026 to 28 February 2027. The nomination form asks for the nominee’s details, a statement of up to 300 words and one supporting link. Self-nominations are welcome. Winners are decided by 20 March 2027 and presented at the ceremonies on 7 and 8 April.

Jury

The jury consists of ACM-W leaders and industry members. Jurors declare any conflict of interest and do not judge nominees from their own institution. Members of the organising committee are not eligible.

FAQ

Who can attend?+

Research scholars, students, faculty members and industry professionals of any gender. Day 1 is designed for research scholars and Day 2 for students, and everyone is welcome on both days.

How much does it cost?+

Attendance is free. Registration is required because places are limited.

Do I need to be a computer science scholar to join the Scholars’ Cohort?+

No. The Scholars’ Cohort is open to research scholars from all disciplines.

Is there a publication or proceedings?+

No. WiCoDE27 is a celebration and mentoring event, not a conference with proceedings. Published or under-review work is welcome in Pitch to Industry, and taking part does not prevent you from publishing your work elsewhere.

How do I become an ACM-W Scholar?+

Apply through the ACM-W Scholars section between 15 November 2026 and 15 January 2027. Selected scholars are announced on 10 February 2027.

Are travel grants available?+

About 20 travel grants for international ACM-W Scholars cover a return flight and three hotel nights, subject to funding. Indicate in your application if you are requesting one.

Can I join the Industry Challenge Sprint on my own?+

Teams have three or four members. If you do not have a team, register your interest and we will help you find one.

Will I receive a certificate?+

Yes. Participants who complete their event passport receive a digital participation certificate.

How do I nominate someone for an award?+

Use the nomination form in the Awards section between 1 December 2026 and 28 February 2027. Self-nominations are welcome.
